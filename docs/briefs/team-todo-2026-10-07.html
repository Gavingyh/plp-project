    File: config/aspects.yaml; command in RUNBOOK.
  • Every model re-run: the adapted encoder fine-tuned on Opus’s labels is the best Done 12 Oct done

    Result: on the first round’s 299 test rows, macro-F1 over three seeds is 0.649 for the adapted encoder fine-tuned on Opus’s labels, 0.631 for FinBERT fine-tuned the same way, 0.621 for the 4 Oct retrain on people’s labels and 0.571 for base FinBERT. The three adapted models averaged reach 0.670 (accuracy 0.716). Negative recall rises from 0.21 (base) to 0.61. On the 100 broker research test rows the adapted set scores 0.740 against base FinBERT’s 0.687, and cross-validation agrees (+0.014, better in 11 of 15 folds). Its lead over FinBERT fine-tuned the same way is about one seed standard deviation; every measure points the same way. This is the model the re-score applies. Two follow-up checks on 12 Oct changed nothing: a neutral threshold chosen on held-out rows came out at zero, and switching on class weights, source weights or both for this model did not improve cross-validation (−0.002 to −0.004 over 15 paired folds).

    Evidence: docs/evidence/model-rerun-2026-10-11.md, with precision and recall for each class on three test scopes; the walk-through is docs/briefs/model-comparison-2026-10-12.html. Models: models/experiments-2026-10-11/dapt/ (not in git).
  • The re-score: rehearsed on a copy, approved, applied, snapshot made Done 12 Oct done

    Result: the database matched the rehearsed copy row for row. The aspect method alone leaves every headline as it was; the model moves them: all time, DBS 0.122 to 0.155, OCBC 0.136 to 0.143, UOB 0.074 to 0.117, and DBS overtakes OCBC. Neutral falls from about two-thirds of sentences to about half, and 29% of labels change. Reddit rises for every bank, CNA turns negative for every bank, and UOB’s broker research falls from 0.236 to −0.026 on 239 sentences, which is worth a person reading. Per-aspect figures change most, because a sentence had 6.4 aspects on average and now has one; some cells rest on under 100 sentences. Base FinBERT’s scores stay in the database for comparison.

    Evidence: docs/evidence/rescore-rehearsal-2026-10-12.md. To undo: data/snapshots/plp-before-rescore-20261012-141607.db.

    What it was: one combined re-run is cheaper and easier to explain than several. scripts/rescore.py applies the LLM aspects and fine-tuned scores (--finetuned --models <folders>); rehearsed on a copy on 3 Oct it reproduced the preview cell for cell. On hold, or nearly done

  • Chatbot speed: free the Mac’s memory before a demo; one option left to check Mostly done 10 Oct before the demo

    Status: measured on 10 Oct, the local model’s speed halved within three minutes as the Mac ran short of memory (11.4 tokens a second down to 5.2, with other programs open), so five questions took 25 to 105 seconds. That is the main cause of slow answers, and closing heavy programs before a demo is the fix. One option is built and left off: writing the tool descriptions where Ollama caches them, instead of having the model read all 967 tokens again for every question (about 10 seconds). On the gold set it was no faster on average (median 36.8 against 41.3 seconds, mean 42.9 against 42.3) and no better on the checks (right tool 22 of 24 against 23), in a run disturbed by other work; rerun it on a quiet Mac before turning it on.

    Evidence: docs/evidence/chatbot-timeout-and-speed-2026-10-10.md. Setting: OLLAMA_TOOLS_IN_SYSTEM.
