Before you use it

Properties of the data that will show up in any cluster, score or chart built on it.

  • DBS dominates.55% of sentences are about DBS, 29% UOB and 16% OCBC. Cross-bank comparisons need per-bank normalisation, and labelling samples should be stratified by bank.
  • Earnings and news read far more positive than Reddit.Base FinBERT scores 25.5% of earnings pairs and 21.7% of news pairs positive, against 2.3% for Reddit. Earnings is management describing its own results; headlines are written to be newsworthy. Earnings is also uneven across banks: DBS 9,093 sentences, OCBC 1,561, UOB 1,600.
  • Reddit is one community.21,794 of the 21,802 Reddit documents come from r/singaporefi. It speaks for that community's retail investors, not Singapore's in general.
  • News is mostly headlines, and its volume over time is a sample.Google News gives headlines only, so most news articles are one sentence. Monthly counts rise towards the present because of Google's ranking, not because coverage grew. About 130 items are self-published (90 from DBS's own newsroom, 41 wire press releases).
  • News has been filtered, and every decision is logged.Articles had to name the bank. Broker and venue names (UOB Kay Hian, DBS Vickers, OCBC Arena) don't count as a mention, and analyst calls on other companies and the banks' daily FX notes were removed. Near-duplicates across sources were dropped. data/news_flagged.csv lists all 3,838 decisions with reasons; details are in docs/news-data.md.
  • The labelling set predates the news.Only 1 of the 997 candidates is a news sentence. If news should be in the fine-tuning data, candidates need re-drawing after aspect assignment has run on it.

How the Google News queries were chosen

Seven pull techniques were piloted on three sample months. Only headline-restricted intitle: queries were worth their requests (27–32 new articles a month for 3 requests); DBS needs a banking context word because a bare "DBS" headline also matches Aston Martin DBS, Dragon Ball Super and American-football "DBs". Weekly windows, other Google editions, per-outlet queries and full-name variants added almost nothing. Final set: three queries per bank.

CNA feed: Emily's scraper (ingest/cna_scraper.py) is in the repo but writes a JSON file, not the database: 0 CNA documents in plp.db, 15 sample articles from 18–20 Sep in ingest/cna_articles.json. 103 CNA headlines are already stored via Google News and Yahoo, so the CNA feed should go through the same filter and dedup when it is loaded.

Almost all of the baseline's polarity sits in the "other" aspect: 45.2% positive and 21.5% negative there, against 3.6% or less for each named aspect. The scorer labels each sentence once and copies the label to every aspect it carries, so this comes from the assignment: sentences that match three or four seed-term centroids at once are plain transactional or factual lines, and evaluative sentences fall below the similarity floor into "other". Per-aspect scores from this taxonomy measure how neutral the matched vocabulary is. Details and options for the taxonomy decision: docs/evidence/aspect-sentiment-concentration-2026-09-24.md.
