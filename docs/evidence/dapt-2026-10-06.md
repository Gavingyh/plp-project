# Domain-adaptive pretraining before fine-tuning, 6 to 7 Oct 2026

FinBERT's masked-word training was continued on the project's own corpus
before fine-tuning, to close the gap between FinBERT's financial English and
the corpus's local product names and Reddit register, where most errors are
([error-analysis-2026-10-01.md](error-analysis-2026-10-01.md): two thirds of
neutral sentences called polar are Reddit, over half retail banking). Slang
was ruled out as the main cause first: with a generous word list (Singlish
particles, "u", "lol", "DCA", emoji), 27 of the 1,280 labelled train/dev rows
(2.1%) hold any (6 Oct, `data/labeled/combined/candidates_resolved.csv`).
**Nothing is adopted:** the database, `config/model_version.py` and the
reported results still use the 1 Oct models. Switching is a team decision.

```bash
HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 .venv-plp/bin/python3 -m train.dapt --out models/dapt-2026-10-06
for seed in 42 1 7; do for f in 0 1 2 3 4; do
  HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 .venv-plp/bin/python3 -m train.finetune --seed $seed --epochs 8 --aspect-text label \
      --folds 5 --fold $f --out-dir models/experiments-2026-10-06/cv [--base-model models/dapt-2026-10-06/classifier]
done; done
HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 .venv-plp/bin/python3 -m train.finetune --seed 42 --epochs 8 --aspect-text label \
    --out-dir models/experiments-2026-10-06/dapt --base-model models/dapt-2026-10-06/classifier   # and seeds 1, 7
```

Run on the M1's GPU under `caffeinate -i`, 6 Oct 20:58 to 7 Oct 03:05
(`data/run/dapt/progress.log`); the CV scores are in
`data/run/dapt/cv_results.jsonl`.

## The pretraining (`train/dapt.py`)

- **Text:** every sentence in `data/plp.db` (44,345, collected to 25 Sep)
  except the 1,277 labelled ones, in every split, and 32 more whose text
  matches a labelled sentence once figures, case and spacing are ignored (a
  results report repeats a sentence each quarter with new figures): 43,036
  sentences, 24,058 of them Reddit. No test text informs training.
- **Model:** FinBERT (`ProsusAI/finbert`) with `bert-base-uncased`'s
  masked-word head, which FinBERT is published without and whose vocabulary
  it shares. 3 epochs, learning rate 5e-5, 15% of tokens masked, sentences
  capped at 64 tokens (96% fit whole), 32 sentences a step, 4,035 steps, 1
  hour 56 minutes. Training loss 7.84 at step 50, 3.95 at step 4,000.
- **Output:** FinBERT's own sentiment head on the adapted encoder, so
  fine-tuning (`--base-model`) runs exactly as it does from FinBERT.
- A first full run on 6 Oct slowed from about 1 s to 100 s a step under
  memory pressure; batches now take two fixed shapes and GPU memory held
  steady at 1.30 GB (commit 1f3fbdf).

## Cross-validation (the decision measure)

Each seed's 5-fold partition was trained twice, from FinBERT and from the
adapted model, so the two see identical folds and their differences are
paired, as in [noise-floor-cv-2026-10-02.md](noise-floor-cv-2026-10-02.md).
Everything else is the 4 Oct retrain's configuration (8 epochs with early
stopping, readable aspect label, plain cross-entropy). The test split was not
used.

| seed | FinBERT, dev macro-F1 | adapted, dev macro-F1 | difference | folds better |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 42 | 0.650 ± 0.037 | 0.702 ± 0.021 | +0.053 | 4 of 5 |
| 1 | 0.665 ± 0.015 | 0.701 ± 0.012 | +0.036 | 4 of 5 |
| 7 | 0.675 ± 0.038 | 0.700 ± 0.035 | +0.025 | 5 of 5 |
| **all 15 folds** | **0.663** | **0.701** | **+0.038** | **13 of 15** |

Over the 15 paired folds the mean difference is +0.038 (sd 0.029; paired
t = 5.01, 14 degrees of freedom, p = 0.0002; Wilcoxon signed-rank p =
0.0003). A 95% t-interval for the effect is +0.022 to +0.054. The two folds
where FinBERT led did so by 0.003 and 0.0003. For scale, the readable-label
change of 2 Oct measured +0.005 on the same design and did not replicate.

## The test split, scored once

Three final models trained from the adapted encoder on all 978 train/dev
rows (seeds 42, 1, 7; early stopping on each seed's 15% dev slice), scored
once on the frozen random test split (299 rows) with `train/evaluate.py`
(`data/run/dapt/test.log`). Against the 4 Oct retrain from FinBERT, the same
configuration ([retrain-2026-10-04.md](retrain-2026-10-04.md)):

| model | test macro-F1, from FinBERT (4 Oct) | test macro-F1, adapted | test accuracy, adapted |
|---|---|---|---|
| base FinBERT | 0.571 | 0.571 | |
| seed 42 | 0.646 | 0.644 | 0.70 |
| seed 1 | 0.592 | 0.602 | 0.69 |
| seed 7 | 0.625 | 0.637 | 0.69 |
| mean ± sd of the seeds | 0.621 ± 0.027 | **0.628 ± 0.022** | |
| average of the three models | 0.634 (accuracy 0.702) | **0.662** (accuracy 0.72) | 0.72 |

The average adapted model's accuracy by source: Reddit 0.68 (184 rows),
earnings 0.80 (79), Google News 0.71 (34).

## What it shows

1. **On cross-validation the gain is clear:** +0.038 dev macro-F1, better in
   13 of 15 paired folds, an interval that excludes zero, and about seven
   times the size of the last change measured this way.
2. **On the test split it is smaller:** the seeds' mean rises by 0.007
   (0.621 to 0.628), within the seed spread (sd 0.022 to 0.027); seed 42 is
   level, seeds 1 and 7 gain 0.010 and 0.012. The average of the three
   models gains more, 0.634 to 0.662 macro-F1 and 0.702 to 0.72 accuracy, its
   first clear lead over base FinBERT on accuracy (0.699).
3. The two measures differ in size, not direction. The CV figure rests on 15
   dev folds of about 196 rows each; the test figure on one 299-row split
   whose labels carry the anchoring caveat of the earlier evidence. The
   honest summary is a consistent improvement whose size on the test split
   is modest for single seeds and larger for the averaged model.

## Caveats

- Only one pretraining run (one seed, one set of settings); the gain could
  move with another.
- The pretraining text is the corpus as collected to 25 Sep; it includes the
  unlabelled sentences the model is later asked to score. That is ordinary
  for adaptive pretraining (no labels are used) and is why the labelled rows
  and their near-copies were left out.
- The models are in `models/dapt-2026-10-06/` and
  `models/experiments-2026-10-06/dapt/seed_*/final` (gitignored). SHA-256 of
  `model.safetensors`, first 16 characters: adapted classifier
  `99aaf085ee5c98fd`; seed 42 `64fd4c1553a2239a`; seed 1 `79418c7ff5a04603`;
  seed 7 `a0b12a659be90453`.
- Adopting them means re-scoring the corpus with the three adapted models
  (`scripts/rescore.py --finetuned --models <folders>`), in the re-score week of 12 to
  18 Oct, as for the 4 Oct retrain.
