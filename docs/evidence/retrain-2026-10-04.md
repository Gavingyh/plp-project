# Retrained models: test results and the full-corpus preview, 4 Oct 2026

The three fine-tuned seeds were retrained after the head-order fix
([training-review-2026-10-03.md](training-review-2026-10-03.md)): the 1 Oct
models started from a pretrained head whose outputs had been renamed, so
each began training answering for the wrong class. The retrain is the 1 Oct
command with a new output folder: the same data, up to 8 epochs (early
stopping ended them after 4, 4 and 3), the aspect's
readable name as the second input (`--aspect-text label`), seeds 42, 1 and 7;
on the GPU, overnight 3 to 4 Oct. The models are in
`models/experiments-2026-10-03/label/seed_*/final` (gitignored; checksums at
the end). **Nothing is adopted:** the database, `config/model_version.py`
and the reported results still use the 1 Oct models. Switching is a team
decision.

```bash
HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 .venv-plp/bin/python3 -m train.finetune --seed 42 --epochs 8 --aspect-text label --out-dir models/experiments-2026-10-03/label   # and seeds 1, 7
HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 .venv-plp/bin/python3 -m train.error_analysis --cpu --models models/experiments-2026-10-03/label/seed_{42,1,7}/final
HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 .venv-plp/bin/python3 -m aggregate.preview --n 44345 --llm-only --models models/experiments-2026-10-03/label/seed_{42,1,7}/final --margin 0.12 --per-seed
HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 .venv-plp/bin/python3 -m aggregate.preview --n 44345 --llm-only --models models/experiments-2026-10-03/label/seed_{42,1,7}/final --per-seed
```

The evaluation used `train/evaluate.py`'s `run(model_dir=[...])` on the CPU.
Training took a few minutes per seed; the full-corpus preview with each seed
scored separately took about two and a half hours on the GPU. The preview
stops if any model's probabilities don't sum to 1; it didn't stop.

## Test results (frozen random test split, 299 rows)

| model | test macro-F1, 1 Oct | test macro-F1, retrained | test accuracy, retrained |
|---|---|---|---|
| base FinBERT | 0.571 | 0.571 | 0.699 |
| seed 42 | 0.630 | **0.646** | 0.709 |
| seed 1 | 0.617 | **0.592** | 0.659 |
| seed 7 | 0.564 | **0.625** | 0.712 |
| mean ± sd of the seeds | 0.604 ± 0.035 | **0.621 ± 0.027** | |
| average of the three models | 0.614 | **0.634** | 0.702 |

**What it shows.**

1. **Every seed now beats base FinBERT.** On 1 Oct seed 7 was below base
   (0.564 against 0.571); retrained, the lowest seed is seed 1 at 0.592. The
   mean rises by 0.017 and the spread between seeds narrows (sd 0.027
   against 0.035). The lead over base, 0.050 on the mean, is 1.4 times the
   1 Oct seed standard deviation (0.035) and 1.9 times this run's (0.027,
   from only three seeds). Seed 1's lead (0.021) is within one standard
   deviation either way.
2. **Seed 1 got worse** (0.617 to 0.592) while seeds 42 and 7 improved, so
   the seed-to-seed noise is still about the size of the gains: three seeds
   can't say how much of the change is the fix.
3. **The average model now matches base on accuracy** (0.702 against
   0.699, one test row), where on 1 Oct it trailed by about 0.04 before the
   margin (0.662). Seed 1 alone is below base on accuracy (0.659).

## The neutral margin now lowers the test score

Each seed's margin was chosen on its own 147 dev rows, as on 1 Oct
([error-analysis-2026-10-01.md](error-analysis-2026-10-01.md)). From
`train.error_analysis`:

| seed | margin chosen on dev | dev macro-F1, argmax → margin | test macro-F1, argmax → margin | neutral called polar | polar called neutral |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 42 | 0.20 | 0.651 → 0.656 | 0.646 → 0.631 | 36 → 29 | 42 → 51 |
| 1 | 0.10 | 0.626 → 0.639 | 0.592 → 0.588 | 44 → 35 | 46 → 51 |
| 7 | 0.05 | 0.644 → 0.646 | 0.625 → 0.594 | 17 → 14 | 62 → 69 |

The average of the three models with the seeds' mean margin (0.12): test
macro-F1 0.634 → 0.612, accuracy 0.702 → 0.702.

**What it shows.** On 1 Oct the models called too many neutral sentences
polar (57 on the average model) and the margin corrected that. The
retrained models make the opposite error more often (average model: 32
neutral called polar, 49 polar called neutral), so the margin, which only
moves sentences towards neutral, makes it worse. The dev gains that chose
the margins are small (0.002 to 0.013 on 147 rows).

This is consistent with the head bug: renamed, the pretrained neutral output
answered for positive, so the 1 Oct models started out calling neutral
sentences positive. It is not proven by this run.

**What this doesn't permit.** The test result can't be used to drop the
margin: no choice of threshold may use the test split. Any rule for keeping
a margin decided now (for example, keep it only if its dev gain is larger
than the noise on 147 rows) has been chosen after seeing this test result,
and must be recorded as such. Both scorings (argmax and margin 0.12) are
reported below.

## The full-corpus preview with the retrained models

All 44,345 sentences, each with its one LLM aspect (scenario D of
[index-preview-2026-10-01.md](index-preview-2026-10-01.md)), scored by the
average of the retrained models. Scenario A (centroid aspects, base
FinBERT) reproduces the stored index; B (LLM aspects, base FinBERT) equals it
on the headlines. Neither involves the retrained models.
Files: `data/preview/index_preview_margin0.12_2026-10-03.csv`,
`index_preview_2026-10-03.csv` (argmax), and the `index_preview_per_seed_*`
versions.

**The headline per bank**

| bank | sentences | base FinBERT (A, B) | 1 Oct models, margin 0.13 | retrained, margin 0.12 | retrained, argmax |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DBS | 24,431 | +0.122 | +0.192 | **+0.132** | +0.143 |
| OCBC | 7,412 | +0.136 | +0.174 | **+0.122** | +0.134 |
| UOB | 12,502 | +0.074 | +0.137 | **+0.084** | +0.097 |

Labels per sentence: base FinBERT 70.0% neutral, 20.3% positive, 9.7%
negative; 1 Oct models with the margin 51.9%, 32.0%, 16.1%; retrained with
the margin 61.8%, 24.7%, 13.5%; retrained, argmax 57.8%, 27.5%, 14.7%.

**Each seed alone (margin 0.12)**

| bank | base FinBERT | seed 1 | seed 42 | seed 7 | spread, retrained | spread, 1 Oct models |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DBS | +0.122 | +0.103 | +0.171 | +0.136 | 0.068 | 0.122 |
| OCBC | +0.136 | +0.100 | +0.162 | +0.110 | 0.062 | 0.094 |
| UOB | +0.074 | +0.080 | +0.121 | +0.062 | 0.060 | 0.044 |

**What it shows.**

1. **The average of the retrained models barely moves the headlines.**
   With the margin it is within 0.015 of base FinBERT (DBS +0.010, OCBC
   −0.014, UOB +0.010); without it within 0.025. The average of the 1 Oct
   models raised every headline by 0.04–0.07. Single seeds differ: retrained
   seed 42 still raises every headline (DBS +0.049, OCBC +0.026, UOB
   +0.047), and seed 1 lowers DBS and OCBC. Whether fine-tuning raises the
   headlines is unsettled; the retrained average doesn't.
2. **DBS and OCBC are too close to order.** Base FinBERT puts OCBC ahead by
   0.014; the retrained average puts DBS ahead by 0.010 (0.009 argmax). DBS
   is ahead under every seed, by 0.003, 0.009 and 0.026. UOB is last under
   every model and seed.
3. **The seeds agree more on the headline** for DBS and OCBC (spreads 0.068
   and 0.062, against 0.122 and 0.094); UOB's spread grows a little (0.060
   against 0.044).
4. **Per aspect, the average of the retrained models agrees with base
   FinBERT on every sign.** All 33 bank-aspect cells (the 30 named aspects plus "other") have
   the same sign as base FinBERT with LLM aspects (B); the 1 Oct models
   differed in 4. The median gap to B is 0.059 (1 Oct models 0.145). The
   negative cells are retail banking for all three banks (DBS −0.103, OCBC
   −0.108, UOB −0.028) and OCBC asset quality (−0.034, 268 sentences), as
   under B. Net interest margin is positive for all three (+0.175 to
   +0.233), where the 1 Oct models had OCBC and UOB negative. Dividends are
   +0.45 to +0.50 (1 Oct models +0.70 to +0.79; base +0.20 to +0.28).
5. **Per aspect, the seeds still disagree.** Across the 30 named cells the
   median spread between seeds is 0.179 (1 Oct 0.183); the largest is DBS
   income and results (0.398). Five cells change sign between seeds (1 Oct:
   eight): asset quality for all three banks, OCBC macro (−0.001 to +0.147)
   and UOB net interest margin (−0.026 to +0.299). Retail banking is
   negative for all three banks under every seed.

## Caveats

- One retraining run per seed; seed noise is as large as the changes.
- The margin was chosen on dev; the test split informed nothing, but the
  test result above shows the dev choice doesn't carry over.
- All-time scores on the current database (base FinBERT's stored labels for
  A and B); the six misdated UOB earnings documents don't affect all-time
  scores.
- Error analysis output names test sentences; it stays in the run's log, not
  in the repository.

## For the team

1. **Adopt the retrained models?** They fix a known training bug, beat base
   FinBERT on macro-F1 under every seed, match it on accuracy (the average),
   and their average agrees with base on every aspect's sign (single seeds
   still disagree on 5 of 30 cells). Adopting them changes the reported Fine-tuning lead
   (0.604 ± 0.035 to 0.621 ± 0.027) everywhere it appears, and the rescore
   step (`scripts/rescore.py`) would then apply them.
2. **Keep the margin?** Decide on dev only (see above).
