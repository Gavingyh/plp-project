How to read this:
  - avg_aspects_per_sentence closer to 1 = more discriminating (candidate A's known
    problem, quantified here — most sentences matching ~4 of 5 aspects near-equally).
  - avg_centroid_separation higher = the aspects are more distinct from each other.
    It does NOT move with the floor: it describes the taxonomy's geometry, so it is
    the one column a floor change cannot flatter.
  - pct_other very high or very low are both worth a second look: too high means the
    taxonomy doesn't fit the corpus; too low (~0%) means the floor never triggers.

This is decision support, not a decision — see config/aspects.yaml's header comment
and the design notes' 'Aspect taxonomy sign-off' open decision.
